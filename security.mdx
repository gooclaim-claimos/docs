This page is for your security and compliance teams. It describes how the platform behaves;
your Gooclaim contact can take you through any part of it in detail.

## Your data, encrypted

- **Member details** — name, date of birth, policy number, mobile number and email — are
  stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). A member is found by a keyed hash of their number or
  address, so the number or address itself is never stored readable.
- **Each organisation has its own encryption key**, protected by its own key in a cloud key
  management service. One organisation's key cannot read another's data.
- **The words in a claim** — a rejection or query reason, a deduction reason, a note on a
  document — are encrypted the same way.
- **Credentials for your systems** (API keys, passwords, certificates) are stored encrypted
  and are never shown again after they are saved.

## Connections to your systems

- Gooclaim OS **reads** your claims and member data; it never writes to your claims system.
  The only thing it writes is a document a member sent, into an SFTP folder you choose.
- Your claims API is reached over `https://` only, signing in the way you choose: API key,
  Basic, OAuth 2.0 client credentials or mutual TLS. See
  [Your claims API](/integration/claims-api#signing-in).
- Before anything is read from your SFTP server, your administrator approves the server's
  identity. If it changes, nothing is read until the new identity is approved.

## Your team's sign-in

- **Two-factor sign-in** (an authenticator app) is required for anyone who administers
  access or handles member data. Recovery codes are issued once.
- **Single sign-on** with your own Microsoft or Google accounts, if you prefer — with the
  option to allow single sign-on only.
- **Sessions** end after 30 minutes without activity and after 12 hours in any case. Signing
  someone out, or changing their role, takes effect on their next action.
- **Passwords** are at least 12 characters and cannot repeat the last five. Repeated failed
  attempts lock the account for a period.
- **An address allowlist** in Portal can restrict sign-in to your office networks.

## Who can see what

- Access is granted by **permission**, through roles your administrator assigns. Changing
  roles or security settings asks for a fresh two-factor code.
- **Access reviews:** your administrator can review who has access and remove what is no
  longer needed; a removal signs the person out at once.
- **Gooclaim support** sees nothing in your organisation until your Tenant Admin or data
  protection officer approves a request. Access to member data needs your data protection
  officer. Support access is read-only, time-limited and recorded.
- **Emergency access** for the Gooclaim team needs a second Gooclaim person to approve it,
  lasts at most four hours, and your administrators and data protection officer are emailed
  when it starts and when it ends.

## Your members

- **Consent comes first.** A member is asked for consent before anything else, as the Digital
  Personal Data Protection Act requires. A member who writes STOP withdraws their consent on
  every channel.
- **Identity before private details.** On channels where a member is not already recognised
  by the mobile number on your records, they enter a one-time code before claim details are
  shared.
- **Erasure requests** are reviewed by your data protection officer, who confirms with their
  password before anything is erased.
- **Retention:** a member's record is removed 90 days after their cover ends (kept while a
  claim is still open). Claim documents are kept for at least 10 years, longer if you choose,
  and a legal hold stops all removal. The audit trail is kept for 10 years.

## AI, with safeguards

- **Members only receive approved message templates.** A model chooses which template and
  which values from your records; it never writes free text to a member. Each template is
  approved by a second person before it can be sent.
- **Every message is checked before it is sent.** Each value in it — a status, an amount, a
  date, a reason — is compared with your claim record, and the message is stopped if any
  value does not match. A check for personal information runs on every message. If the check
  cannot run, the message is not sent.
- **Every AI call goes through one gateway**, which records usage against your organisation.
  AI models are provided by third-party model providers, reached only through that gateway.
- **Orion's answers to your staff** are checked before they are shown.

## Documents members send

- Members send documents only through a secure upload link.
- A file's type is decided from its contents, not its name; PDF, JPEG, PNG and HEIC are
  accepted.
- Every file is scanned for malware. A file with no scan result is never treated as clean.

## The audit trail

- Every automated decision is recorded: what was decided, by which workflow, and when.
- Each organisation's records are chained with a cryptographic hash, so a changed or removed
  record can be detected, and the records cannot be edited in place.
- Your compliance team can search the trail and export it as CSV or JSON in Portal.

## Separation between organisations

Which organisation a request belongs to is taken from the verified sign-in, never from the
request itself. A request naming another organisation is refused and recorded.
