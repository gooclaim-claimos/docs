---
title: "Getting started"
description: "What has to be ready before your organisation goes live on Gooclaim OS, who does each step, and how testing and go-live work."
---

Your organisation goes live once four things are ready. Some steps are yours, done in
**Portal**; the rest the Gooclaim team sets up for you. Your Portal dashboard shows where
each one stands.

## 1. Your team can sign in

- The Gooclaim team invites your **first administrator**.
- Your administrator invites the rest of the team in Portal and gives each person a role.
- Anyone who handles member data or administers access signs in with a second factor (an
  authenticator app).
- If you prefer, your team can sign in with your own **Microsoft** or **Google** account.

## 2. Members can be recognised

Gooclaim OS recognises a member by the mobile number or email address on your records.

- **You** send your member file — uploaded in Portal, or collected from your SFTP server.
- **Gooclaim** sets up an encryption key for your organisation. Member details are stored
  encrypted with it, and a member is looked up without their number or address ever being
  stored readable.

## 3. Members can reach you

Members write in on **WhatsApp** or **Email**. The Gooclaim team switches on the channels you
want; then:

- **Email:** you choose the address replies come from (for example, an address on your own
  domain), publish the DNS records Portal shows you, and press **Verify**.
- **WhatsApp:** you connect your WhatsApp Business number in Portal.

## 4. Claims can be answered

- **Your claims data.** Gooclaim OS reads your claims from where they already are, in one of
  three ways: your claims system's API, a file on your SFTP server, or claim files you upload
  or we collect on a schedule. The Gooclaim team maps your field names to Gooclaim OS once,
  and that mapping is checked before it is used.
- **The questions it answers.** The Gooclaim team switches on the workflows you want: claim
  status, pending documents, and why a claim was rejected or queried.
- **Your wording.** Members only ever receive approved message templates. Your templates are
  set up for you, and you can review them in Portal.
- **Languages.** Replies go out in English, Hindi or Hinglish — the ones switched on for
  your organisation. A member is answered in the language they wrote in when it is switched
  on, and otherwise in your default language, which you choose in Portal.
- **AI budget.** A monthly limit caps your organisation's AI use. Portal shows what you
  have used.

## Testing

Before anyone else can reach you, there is a testing period:

1. Only **test contacts** — a few of your own people, registered by the Gooclaim team — get
   answers. They are answered with your real claim data, exactly as members will be.
2. Each channel and each language you have switched on needs at least one completed test
   conversation.
3. Your administrator confirms in Portal that the test conversations looked right.

## Going live

The Gooclaim team turns your organisation live once the four steps are ready, the test
conversations are complete and your administrator has confirmed them. From then on, any
member on your records can write in. Every go-live is recorded: who pressed it, when, and
what was checked.
