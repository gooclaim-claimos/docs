---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The words used across Gooclaim OS and this site."
---

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Gooclaim OS** | A Claim OS: it connects to your claims systems, answers your members on their channels, and gives your claims team an AI coworker, with every automated step recorded. |
| **Portal** | Where your administrators manage your organisation: team and access, channels, message templates, data connections, reports, usage and compliance. |
| **Orion** | The AI claims coworker for your claims staff. It reads the claim, prepares the next step with the evidence, and waits for a person to approve it. |
| **Member** | A person covered by a policy you administer — the person who writes in about a claim. |
| **Member file** | The list of your members you send to Gooclaim OS, so a member can be recognised by the mobile number or email address on your records. |
| **Claim record** | Your organisation's own record of a claim. Every answer about a claim comes from it. |
| **Claims connection** | How Gooclaim OS reads your claim records: your claims system's API, a file on your SFTP server, or claim files you upload or we collect on a schedule. |
| **Field mapping** | The one-time translation from your field names and codes to Gooclaim OS's. A record that does not fit the mapping is not guessed at; it is stopped and reported. |
| **Channel** | Where members write in and get replies: WhatsApp or Email. |
| **Workflow** | One kind of question Gooclaim OS can answer, such as claim status, pending documents, or why a claim was rejected or queried. |
| **Message template** | An approved wording for a reply. Members only ever receive approved templates, never free text written by a model. |
| **Consent** | A member's agreement to the processing of their data, asked for before anything else, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires. |
| **One-time code** | A code sent to the mobile number on your records. A member enters it before a private detail is shared. |
| **Secure upload link** | A link a member receives when a claim is waiting on documents. Files sent through it are scanned before anyone sees them. |
| **Escalation** | A conversation handed to your claims staff because a person is needed. It reaches them in Orion. |
| **Erasure request** | A member's request to have their data erased. Your data protection officer reviews it in Portal, and a second person carries it out. |
| **Test contact** | One of your own people allowed to write in during testing, before your organisation goes live. |
| **Go live** | The moment your organisation starts answering every member on your records, after testing. |
| **Audit trail** | The record of every automated decision Gooclaim OS makes for your organisation, which your compliance team can search and export. |
| **AI budget** | The monthly limit on your organisation's AI use. |
