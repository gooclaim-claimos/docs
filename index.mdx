**Gooclaim OS is a Claim OS.** It connects to the systems where your claims already live,
talks to your members on the channels they use, and puts an AI coworker next to your claims
team — with every automated step recorded.

It is built for **TPAs and insurers**. Health insurance is the first sector it serves.

## What your members get

Members write in on **WhatsApp** or **Email**. Replies go out in **English, Hindi or
Hinglish**. A member who writes in another Indian language is told, in their own script,
that help in that language is not available yet.

## What keeps it safe

- **Consent first.** Before anything else, a member is asked for consent to process their
  data, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires. A member can withdraw it, and
  can ask for their data to be erased.
- **Identity before private details.** Before a private detail is shared, the member proves
  they hold the mobile number on your records with a one-time code.
- **Only approved wording.** Members receive your approved message templates, never free
  text written by a model.
- **Checked against your records.** Every reply is checked before it is sent: the facts in
  it must match your claim record. A reply that cannot be verified is not sent; the member
  gets a holding reply instead.
- **Everything recorded.** Every automated decision goes into an audit trail your
  compliance team can search and export.
