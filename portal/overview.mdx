Portal is where your administrators run your organisation on Gooclaim OS.

## Signing in

- **Invitation.** You join from an email invitation. The link is valid for 7 days. You set
  a password and, the first time you sign in, turn on two-step verification.
- **Password.** At least 12 characters, with an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol.
  **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page emails a reset link that works for 15 minutes.
  Resetting your password signs you out everywhere else.
- **Two-step verification.** Scan the QR code with an authenticator app and enter the
  6-digit code. Save the recovery codes you are shown: each works once, if you lose your
  phone.
- **Company sign-in.** If your organisation has switched on single sign-on, you sign in with
  your Microsoft or Google work account instead. See
  [Team and access](/portal/team-and-access#single-sign-on).

## Finding your way around

| Section | What is in it |
|---------|---------------|
| **Dashboard** | The last 30 days at a glance, items that need attention, and your go-live status |
| **Data & Connectors** | How your claims and member data reach Gooclaim OS |
| **Interaction channels** | WhatsApp and Email for members, and alerts for your team |
| **Templates** | The approved wording members receive |
| **Performance** | Requests handled, and how document requests are going |
| **Usage** | Member conversations, Orion use and your AI limit |
| **Compliance** | Complaints, erasure requests, consent records, the IRDAI turnaround report and blocked members |
| **Audit log** | Every automated decision, searchable and exportable |
| **Organisation** | Members and roles, your organisation's settings, alerts and security |
| **Help & support** | How to reach Gooclaim support |

Search Portal with **⌘K**. The bell opens your notifications.

## Roles and permissions

Each person has one or more roles, and a role is a set of permissions. Portal shows each
person only the sections and buttons their permissions allow. If someone opens a page they
cannot use, Portal tells them which permission they need and offers **Ask for access**.

Built-in roles include Tenant Admin, Claims Head, Team Lead, Claims Processor, Medical
Officer, QA Auditor, Fraud Analyst, Grievance Officer, DPO / Compliance, Integration Admin
and Viewer. Your administrator can also create custom roles.

<Note>
  For sensitive changes, Portal asks you to confirm it is you with a fresh two-step code if
  your last one is more than 15 minutes old.
</Note>
