## Organisation

Under **Organisation → Organisation**:

- **Tenant profile:** upload your logo (PNG or JPG, at least 128 px on the long side, up to
  512 KB), see your organisation's identity and licence details, and choose **your default
  language** for replies.
- **Channels** and **Workflows:** which are switched on for you. To change them, contact
  your Gooclaim onboarding team.
- **Operational mode:** Operational, Restricted or Suspended.

## Notifications

The bell opens your notifications. Filter by unread, action needed, system or compliance;
mark them read, or open the item they point to. **Notification settings** turns each
category on or off in Portal.

## Your account

Open your name in the top bar for **User settings**:

- **Profile:** your first and last name. Your email address can only be changed by your
  administrator.
- **Security:** change your password; doing so signs you out on every other device.
- **Two-factor:** set up your authenticator app, make new recovery codes, or turn it off
  (with your password and a current code).
- **Active sessions:** see where you are signed in, and sign out any device, or all others.
