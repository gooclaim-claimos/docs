Open **Interaction channels**. Each channel shows whether it is **Not enabled**, **Not
connected** or **Active**. The Gooclaim team switches a channel on; you then connect it.

## WhatsApp

When WhatsApp has been switched on for you, connect your WhatsApp Business number with:

- WhatsApp Business account ID
- Phone number ID
- Access token — use a **System User** token; a temporary token stops working within a day
- App secret

Press **Connect**. The token and secret are never shown again. Until the number is
connected, nothing sent to it reaches you.

## Email

</Frame>

Every organisation gets a claims address from Gooclaim, shown on the Email card with
**Copy**. Forward your own claims address to it and it works at once.

To have replies go out **from your own address** (for example, an address on your domain):

1. Enter the address members write to and **Save**.
2. Add the DNS records Portal shows for your domain, then press **Verify**. You do not need
   to change your MX or SPF records. DNS changes can take a few hours.
3. Send a copy of that mailbox's mail to Gooclaim. Portal has steps for Gmail, Microsoft 365
   and Zoho.
4. **Send test** to check it end to end.

Changing the address later clears the verification.

## Members' AI use

Set how much of your organisation's AI use one member may take per day, as a percentage.
When a member reaches it, they are told your team will be in touch, and your team gets one
ticket that day.

## Alerts for your team

Team channels send alerts to your own staff, not to members. Set them up under
**Organisation → Alerts**:

- Everyone with the Admin role always sees alerts in the Portal inbox (the bell).
- Choose a default channel — **Microsoft Teams**, **Slack** or **Email** — save it, then
  **Send test**.
- Each kind of alert can be switched on or off and sent to extra destinations.
