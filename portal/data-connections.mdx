Open **Data & Connectors**. **Data Sources** holds your knowledge library; **Connectors**
brings in your claims and members.

## Data sources

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/portal-data-sources.webp" alt="Data Sources: open Gooclaim Sources to connect document libraries such as SharePoint, Google Drive or Confluence" />
</Frame>

**Open Gooclaim Sources** opens a separate app, in a new tab, where you connect your document
libraries — for example SharePoint, Google Drive or Confluence — and upload files. They become
your knowledge library: Orion searches it when your staff ask a question, and cites the
passage it used.

## Connectors

Under **Connectors** there are two ways in: a **CMS connector** that reads your claims
system, and **Batch Dump** for files.

### CMS connectors

The list shows each connector, its field mapping (**Draft**, **Active** or **Paused**), its
health and its last successful fetch.

**Add CMS Connector** walks you through three steps:

1. **Connect.** Choose the connector type, enter your claims system's `https://` address,
   and choose how Gooclaim OS signs in: **API key**, **Basic**, **OAuth 2.0 client
   credentials** or **Mutual TLS** (see [Your claims API](/integration/claims-api#signing-in)).
   For an SFTP server, press **Test connection**, check the server's fingerprint, and **Approve this server** before
   anything is read. If the server's identity later changes, nothing is read until you
   approve the new one.
2. **Sample data.** Upload or paste a sample of your records (CSV or JSON).
3. **Submit.** Your credentials are saved and your sample goes to the Gooclaim onboarding
   team, who build and test the field mapping.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[You: connect and send a sample] --> B[Gooclaim builds the field mapping]
    B --> C[Mapping tested]
    C --> D[Gooclaim activates it]
    D --> E[Claims are read]
```

The connector stays in **Draft** until Gooclaim activates its mapping. A record that does
not fit the mapping is never guessed at: it is stopped and reported.

<Note>To change a connector's address or credentials, contact your Gooclaim onboarding contact.</Note>

### Batch Dump (files)

Send member and claim files by SFTP, or upload them by hand.

- **By SFTP:** after setup, Gooclaim checks your server for new files every hour. See
  [Files and SFTP](/integration/files) for the folders and rules.
- **By hand:** choose the file type (**Members**, **Claims**, **Claim documents** or **Claim
  deductions**) and a
  `.csv` or `.xlsx` file, press **Preview** to see what would be stored — nothing is saved
  yet — then **Looks right — ingest it**.

The **last sync** table shows each file and what happened to it: Synced, Mapped nothing to
store, Rejected, Store failed or Processing failed. Uploading a file that has already been
synced changes nothing.
