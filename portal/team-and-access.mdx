All of this is under **Organisation → Members & Roles** and **Organisation → Security**.

## Members

The **Members** tab lists everyone in your organisation, with their roles and whether
two-step verification is on.

- **Edit roles** changes a person's roles.
- **Unlock** lets a person back in after too many failed sign-ins.
- If only one person holds the Tenant Admin role, Portal suggests giving it to a second
  trusted person.

## Invitations

**Invite member** sends an email invitation with a role (Viewer unless you choose
another). Pending invitations can be resent or revoked; expired ones can be sent again.

## Roles

The **Roles & Permissions** tab lists the built-in and custom roles. **Create custom role**
makes a new role from the permissions you tick.

## Access reviews

Portal schedules two reviews: **admin rights every 3 months** and **everyone every 6
months**.

1. Start a review.
2. For each person, choose **Keep** or **Remove**. Removing suspends and signs them out at
   once.
3. **Complete the review** once every line is decided.

Nobody decides their own line. A finished review can be downloaded as CSV or printed.

## Access history

A read-only record of role changes, invitations, sign-in IP list changes, lockouts, access
reviews and any Gooclaim access to your account.

## Gooclaim support access

Gooclaim has no standing access to your organisation. If Gooclaim support needs to look at
your account, they ask first:

- Your Tenant Admin or DPO approves the request for a set time, or refuses it.
- A request that includes claims and member data can only be approved by your DPO.
- Access is read-only and ends by itself. You can stop it at any time with **Stop now**.
- While access is open, an amber bar at the top of Portal says who can see your account and
  until when.

In an emergency, Gooclaim may open read-only access without asking first; you are emailed
at once, and it appears in your access history.

## Single sign-on

Under **Organisation → Security**, your administrator can let your team sign in with
**Microsoft** (Entra ID / Office 365) or **Google Workspace**:

1. Choose your company login, and whether a first-time user joins as a Viewer or is refused
   unless invited.
2. Add your email domains and prove you own them with a DNS TXT record.
3. For Microsoft, approve the Gooclaim app in Microsoft.
4. Test sign-in with your own account.
5. Switch single sign-on on.

You can also map Microsoft groups to Gooclaim roles. With **SSO only**, passwords stop
working for everyone except one or two break-glass Tenant Admins you choose.

Someone you disable at your company loses access to Gooclaim within 12 hours.

## Sign-in IP list

Limit sign-in to the addresses and ranges you list (IPv4 and IPv6). Your own current
address must stay on the list, so you cannot lock yourself out. Every change shows in your
access history.

<Note>Only your own administrators can change single sign-on and the IP list — Gooclaim cannot.</Note>
