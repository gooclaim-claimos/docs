Members only ever receive approved message templates. Open **Templates**.

## Registry

The registry lists every template for your organisation, curated and approved by Gooclaim.
Filter by category (Consent, Claim status, Document requests, Claim inquiries, Grievance,
Other), channel and language. Open a template to see, for each channel and language,
whether Gooclaim has approved it, whether the channel's authority has approved it, and
whether it can be sent.

## Submission tracker

WhatsApp templates also need Meta's approval before they can be sent. In **Submission
tracker**:

1. Tick the wordings to send (those not yet sent, refused, paused or switched off).
2. Press **Submit**.
3. Track each one: Under review, Refused, Paused, Switched off or Appeal open. A refusal
   shows Meta's reason word for word.

<Tip>Start with a handful. Each refusal means a rewrite and another wait.</Tip>
