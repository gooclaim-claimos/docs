Open a claim from Work Pulse or **Find a claim**.

## At the top

- **The IRDAI clock** — days left, or how far past the deadline it is, with the estimated
  interest running. Hover for the rule it is counted under.
- **Highlights** — the member, your claims record and when it last changed, the amounts
  claimed and approved, what was paid and when, how many documents are with you, what the claim is waiting on, the
  hospital and the policy, and where the claim stands: Submitted, Documents, Review,
  Decision.

## Overview

- **Holding this claim** — what is in the way, from the sources Orion reads.
- **Decision** — for a rejected claim, the reason as your record states it.
- **Payment** — what was paid, when, and the payment reference (UTR or cheque number); the
  pre-authorisation, if there is one; and each deduction, in your record's own words, with its
  amount. If less was approved than claimed and your record gives no reason, Orion says so —
  IRDAI requires the reasons for a deduction to reach the member.
- **Complaints on this claim** — each complaint's deadline or outcome, and whether the
  member was told.
- **Since your last visit** — what changed.
- **What Orion cannot show** — anything not on your record, not readable just now, not
  connected, or not visible to you.

Your claims system remains the record. Orion shows what it can verify.

## Documents

Each document the claim needs, with its status — with you, sent by the member, requested,
missing or rejected — and the files the member sent through the secure link underneath.

- Files are scanned before anyone can open them. A held or refused file shows why, and
  cannot be opened.
- Orion may point out a mismatch — "sent as one document but reads like another", or "the
  patient name does not match this claim's member" — and signals worth a look, such as the
  same file sent on another claim or a PDF edited after it was made. These are signals, not
  proof: the decision is yours.
- Files open in a viewer with zoom and rotate. Opening a file is recorded. Downloading needs
  the claim to be in your own work.

## Conversation

The member's conversation, newest first, kept as part of the claim record (identity, card
and bank numbers are masked), and every message Gooclaim sent automatically, with how each
conversation ended.

## History

A dated timeline. Each line says whose record it came from: yours, Gooclaim's or Orion's.
