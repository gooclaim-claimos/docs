---

On every claim, Orion sits in the panel on the right.

## The next safe step

Orion opens by saying what is holding the claim, then suggests what it would do next, with
the reason and numbered evidence you can click through to. Depending on the claim, that
might be:

- escalate it to a senior, because the IRDAI deadline has passed;
- hand it to someone, because nobody holds it;
- review a document the member just sent;
- ask the member again for documents still owed.

Press **Yes** to do it, **Someone else** to choose another person, **Not now** (already done,
not right for this claim, or later), or **Why?** to ask. Orion never suggests approving,
rejecting or paying.

## Asking Orion

Ask about the claim in plain words — "Summarise this claim", "Which documents are still
needed?", "Why was it rejected?". Orion reads the claim's record, its documents list, the
clock, complaints and the member's recent messages, and can search your organisation's
knowledge library.

- Every answer lists its **sources**. A passage from your library opens at the page, with
  the quoted words boxed.
- Nothing is sent to a member from the chat.
- Orion does not read the contents of files members upload.
- The chat starts fresh each time you open a claim.

You can mark an answer **Helpful**, **Incorrect**, **Citation issue**, or give a
**Correction**.

## What Orion holds back

Before you see an answer, it is checked. An answer is held back if it would show an identity
number in full, name a different claim, state an amount or date that is not on the record,
quote words a document does not contain, lean towards a claim decision, or mislead. You see
that it was held, and can ask again.

## Prepared actions

When an answer leads to an action, Orion prepares it as a card — hand over, escalate, or ask
the member. A card changes nothing until you press **Yes**; it is checked again when you
press, pressing twice does it once, and every action is recorded. Cards expire after 8
hours.

You can also open **Claim actions** yourself:

- **Ask the member again** — shows exactly what the member will get (your approved
  reminder in their language, the documents still owed and a secure upload link) before you
  send it.
- **Hand to someone else** — Orion suggests the person on your team with the fewest open
  claims.
- **Escalate to a senior** — with a short reason the person reads.
- **Mark in progress**, **Waiting docs** or **Complete**. After completing, record the final
  outcome your team decided. Completed work cannot be reopened.

## Your monthly share

Your administrator can give each person a share of your organisation's monthly AI limit. The
ring beside the question box shows how much of yours you have used. You are told at 75%; at
100%, asking Orion pauses until the 1st of next month. Your administrator can change your
share in Portal.
